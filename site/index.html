How a round works

Ten minutes. One dish. Last cell wins.

Every round starts with the whole dish open. Grow, hunt and survive while the safe zone shrinks toward the centre.

01

Absorb

Eat food and anything smaller than you to grow. Bigger cells are slower, so size is power and a target at once.

02

Divide to strike

Split to launch half your mass at a cell in reach, shoot mass to bait or feed, and boost out of trouble.

03

Outlast the dish

The arena closes in and anything outside dissolves. When only one player's cells are left, the round is theirs.

Questions

Good to know

Is Mitosis free?

Yes. Every mode is free to play. ATP, the game's currency, is earned by playing; in the apps you can also buy ATP packs if you want a shortcut. There are no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. You can tap Play straight away and your progress is kept on that device. Sign in with Apple or Google when you want it on other devices too.

Can I play with friends?

Add each other with a friend code, make a party and you'll land in the same arena, whether you're on the web, iPhone or Android.

What do I need to play in a browser?

A recent Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox on a computer, tablet or phone. Use the mouse (or a finger) to steer, Space to split, W to shoot mass and Shift to boost.

Can I play offline?

Practice against bots at Easy, Normal, Hard or Expert works without a connection. Ranked and live rounds need the internet.

How do I delete my account?

In the game: Settings › Account › Delete account. It's removed from our server straight away. More on deleting your data.
