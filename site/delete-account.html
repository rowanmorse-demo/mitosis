Delete your account

You can delete your Mitosis account and its data at any time. This applies to the web game, the iPhone app and the Android app (Mitosis: Cell Arena, by Catapult Consulting, LLC).

In the game (straight away)

  1. Open Mitosis and go to Settings › Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is removed from our server immediately: rating, XP and level, stats and trophies, ATP, skins, battle pass progress, run history, display name, and any Sign in with Apple or Google link. The game then clears its data on that device and starts you fresh.

Records of App Store and Google Play purchases are kept only as anonymous transaction ids, so a purchase can't be credited twice. They are no longer linked to you.

If you can't open the game

Email support@mitosisgame.com from any address and tell us which account to delete: the display name, roughly when you last played, and the platform. If you signed in with Apple or Google, say which. We complete deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

Signing out instead

Signing out (Settings › Account › Sign out) detaches a device from your account without deleting anything. The account keeps your progress, and you can sign in again later.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy.
