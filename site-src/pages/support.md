# Support

Something wrong, or a question about Mitosis? We read everything.

## Get in touch

- **Email:** support@mitosisgame.com
- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue at https://github.com/rowanmorse-demo/mitosis/issues

Tell us your device (web browser, iPhone or Android), the app version from Settings, and what happened. For a purchase, include the order number from your App Store or Google Play receipt.

## Common questions

### I bought ATP but it didn't arrive

Purchases are checked with Apple or Google before the ATP is added, which usually takes a second or two. If you were offline, open the game again with a connection and it is added then. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with your receipt's order number.

### How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Sign in with Apple or Google on the old device (Settings › Account), then sign in with the same account on the new one. Your ATP, skins, rating and level come with you, on the web, iPhone and Android.

### Refunds

Purchases in the apps go through the App Store or Google Play, so refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google (in the Google Play app, under Payments & subscriptions › Budget & history).

### The game is laggy

Live rounds pick the closest server we have, and Settings › Max ping keeps you out of rounds where your connection is too slow. On older phones, Settings › Effects › Reduced and Battery saver help.
