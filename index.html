      Pause
      Bottom centre, with a Return to lobby option
      Rounds Ten minutes, two lives. The arena closes in from the one-minute mark and is down to its final size by 9:30; anything outside dissolves. Bots stop respawning at 7:00, and the round ends the moment one player is left. ATP pays for placing: 25 for a win, down to 1 for 30th. Mutate at mass milestones — a random trait is applied, six times a life, and every cell wears badges for the traits it has. World events: nutrient blooms, antibiotic tides that close the well, phage swarms and the Leviathan — lure it onto phages to break it up. The leader wears a crown and pays a bounty. Hunter phages break in through the rim and inject any cell they touch — it bursts into up to 16 pieces 8–10 s later. Nothing is immune to them; three ejected blobs kill one. Practice plays the bots at the difficulty you pick, and on iPhone, Nearby plays the phones around you with no internet. Tap the mass readout or the clock in a game to shrink them, or the HUD button to hide the on-screen info.
